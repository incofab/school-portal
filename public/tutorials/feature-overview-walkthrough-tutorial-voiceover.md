# EduManager Feature Overview Walkthrough

## Introduction

Welcome to EduManager. In the next few minutes, we’ll take a read-only tour of the main places where a school manages its day-to-day work. We’ll sign in to the school administrator account, look around the dashboard, and move through the student, academic, payment, communication, curriculum, and examination areas. This is an overview, so we’re only looking at the pages and what they are used for.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is the starting point for the school. It brings together quick access to people, classes, subjects, results, payments, and other operational areas. The sidebar keeps the wider application close at hand, so an administrator can move from a high-level overview into the part of the school day that needs attention.

## Student Management

The Students area is where administrators see the school’s registered learners. This list brings together student names, classes, identification details, registration dates, and related row actions. Search and filters make it easier to find the right record, while the related student and class tools lead to profiles, guardians, payments, results, evaluations, identification outputs, and class movement or promotion workflows.

## Fees and Payments

The Fees page is the starting point for the school’s fee structure. Each row can show the fee title, amount, line items, sectors, and payment interval. The payment area then gives the school a record of payment activity, along with totals and filters for reviewing what has been paid. Related receipt and student payment-history pages provide a convenient way to review receipts when records are available.

## Attendance

Attendance brings several views together. The attendance list provides a record of daily entries and filters. The recording page is the staff-facing workspace used during a normal attendance session, while the class register gives a class-level view for a selected date. The student attendance report makes it possible to review attendance over an academic period without changing the underlying records.

## Results and Score Management

EduManager’s result system connects course results, class analysis, term results, session results, reports, and publication review. Course result pages organize subject-level academic records. Class result analysis brings a class picture together for review. Term results show grades, totals, positions, comments, and related student details when records are present, and a Result Detail link can open a read-only student result record.

Session Results collect the academic picture across the school session. Where a session record is available, its Result Sheet link opens a report-style view for reading or printing. The Result Publications area is where administrators can review publication records and report outputs. No score is entered, calculated, locked, transferred, or published in this overview.

## Result Templates and Result Settings

The settings page includes a visible Result Setting section. Here, the school can review preferences such as how positions are displayed, which result template is selected, and how exam results appear. The Preview Template link opens a report-sheet preview so the school can understand the presentation of a result before using the settings in a real working session. The current choices are left unchanged in this tour.

## Messages and Communication

The message list provides a place to review communication history. The composition page shows the available recipient, channel, subject, and message areas, without entering a message or selecting a recipient. Notifications provide another communication view, including sent notification history when records are available. Together, these areas support communication with students, guardians, staff, and groups.

## Fee Reminders

Back on the fee list, a populated fee row can show the Send Reminder control. That is where a school can begin following up on an outstanding payment with the relevant recipients. We’re only pointing out where the control lives; no reminder panel is opened and no reminder is sent.

## Assignments and Submissions

The Assignments area helps teachers organize student work, instructions, and due dates. The assignment list provides the overview, and a View link opens assignment details when a record is available. The Submitted Assignments page is the review list for student work, with a read-only View path for an individual submission when one exists. This walkthrough does not create an assignment, upload work, score a submission, or change any record.

## Lesson Plans, Lesson Notes, and Curriculum

The curriculum section starts with Schemes of Work, which provide the broader teaching structure. Lesson Plans then organize what a teacher intends to teach, and a View link can open a read-only plan detail when a plan is present. Lesson Notes document what was taught and keep supporting material structured for future reference, with the same read-only detail option when a note exists. Topics provide another layer for organizing teaching content across subjects and plans.

## Supporting Areas

A few shorter areas complete the picture. Classes and class groups provide the structure used by students, attendance, fees, and results. Subjects are maintained in the academic area, alongside related teacher-assignment tools. Report pages turn academic records into focused views for subjects, classes, grades, and individual review.

## CBT Exams

EduManager also gives institutions a place to manage computer-based tests. CBT Events can support internal school exams, admission exams, and recruitment tests, with connected subjects and question content reviewed from the event area. If an existing event is available, its View path opens the exam overview. This tour does not create an exam or start an active attempt.

## Conclusion

That completes our EduManager feature overview. From students and attendance to results, payments, communication, curriculum, and CBT exams, the school’s daily operations are connected in one workspace. This tour stayed read-only so you could see where each feature lives before using the tools in a real working session. Thanks for watching.
